Art Carved Jewelry

Role: In-House Designer, Friedrick Goldman Scope: Print & Email Campaign Design

Overview

Art Carved is a heritage jewelry brand with decades of history in the bridal and engagement ring market. As an in-house designer at Friedrick Goldman — the parent company behind multiple jewelry brands — I contributed creative direction and design across print and digital campaigns for Art Carved.

The Work

Working across a portfolio of brands under the Friedrick Goldman umbrella, I helped develop campaign creative that spanned magazine advertising and email marketing. For Art Carved, one of the most meaningful projects was contributing to the creative for their same-sex engagement ring campaign — visual storytelling that reflected a more inclusive vision of love and commitment at a time when that representation mattered deeply in the bridal category.

The work required balancing Art Carved’s classic, refined aesthetic with a fresh, culturally resonant perspective — honoring the brand’s heritage while expanding who it spoke to.

Impact

The campaign creative ran across national magazine placements and email marketing, reaching Art Carved’s broad customer base with imagery that was both brand-aligned and culturally forward.

Disciplines

Print Art Direction · Email Design · Campaign Creative · Bridal & Lifestyle Marketing

Musiqology — Brand Identity


Role: In-House Creative Scope: Brand Identity Design


Overview
Musiqology was an editorial platform celebrating music, culture, and scholarship — anchored by the work of Dr. Guthrie Ramsey and the artists in his orbit. Before the platform could publish a single word, it needed a visual identity that could carry the weight of that mission.


The Work
I developed the brand identity system for Musiqology — logo, color palette, typography, and the overall look and feel — and extended that system into promotional graphics for Dr. Ramsey’s live shows and events.

That last part was critical. A brand that only lives on a website is incomplete — Musiqology needed to show up in the real world, on stage, and in the spaces where the music actually happened. I designed promotional materials that carried the brand’s visual language into event contexts, creating consistency across digital and physical touchpoints.

The design challenge throughout was finding the right balance: credible and culturally serious without being stiff, energetic without being shallow. The identity drew from the richness of Black music tradition while feeling contemporary and forward-looking.


Legacy
Though the original platform is no longer active, the brand evolved into Musiqology Media — and the foundational identity work created during this period continues to inform the platform’s visual direction today.


Disciplines
Brand Identity · Logo Design · Typography · Color Systems · Event Promotion · Print Design · Art Direction

Musiqology — Editorial Design

Role: In-House Creative Scope: Website & Editorial Design

Overview

Musiqology was a music and culture editorial platform built around the scholarship and artistry of Dr. Guthrie Ramsey and collaborating artists. As the in-house creative, I was responsible for designing the full digital editorial experience — from homepage to article layout.

The Work

I designed the end-to-end website experience for Musiqology, creating a platform that could present music journalism and cultural commentary with the visual sophistication the content deserved.

The scope included homepage design, full site design, and article/content layout — each requiring its own creative thinking. The homepage had to draw readers in and signal the platform’s point of view immediately. Article layouts had to be clean and readable while still feeling distinctly Musiqology. Together, the site functioned as both a publication and a cultural statement.

The editorial design system was built to be consistent enough to feel like a cohesive publication, and flexible enough to serve a wide range of content — essays, interviews, reviews, and artist features.

Legacy

The editorial framework developed for Musiqology laid the groundwork for the platform’s evolution into Musiqology Media, demonstrating the lasting impact of intentional design systems built for scale.

This project is part of a broader creative system developed for Musiqology — see also: Musiqology Brand Identity.

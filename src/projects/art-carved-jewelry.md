<h4 class="text-text-100 mt-3 -mb-1 text-[1.375rem] font-bold">Art Carved Jewelry</h4>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]"><strong>Role:</strong> In-House Designer, Friedrick Goldman <strong>Scope:</strong> Print &amp; Email Campaign Design</p>
<hr class="border-border-200 border-t-0.5 my-3 mx-1.5" />
<p class="text-text-100 mt-3 -mb-1 text-[1.125rem] font-bold">Overview</p>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]">Art Carved is a heritage jewelry brand with decades of history in the bridal and engagement ring market. As an in-house designer at Friedrick Goldman — the parent company behind multiple jewelry brands — I contributed creative direction and design across print and digital campaigns for Art Carved.</p>
<hr class="border-border-200 border-t-0.5 my-3 mx-1.5" />
<p class="text-text-100 mt-3 -mb-1 text-[1.125rem] font-bold">The Work</p>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]">Working across a portfolio of brands under the Friedrick Goldman umbrella, I helped develop campaign creative that spanned magazine advertising and email marketing. For Art Carved, one of the most meaningful projects was contributing to the creative for their same-sex engagement ring campaign — visual storytelling that reflected a more inclusive vision of love and commitment at a time when that representation mattered deeply in the bridal category.</p>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]">The work required balancing Art Carved&#8217;s classic, refined aesthetic with a fresh, culturally resonant perspective — honoring the brand&#8217;s heritage while expanding who it spoke to.</p>
<hr class="border-border-200 border-t-0.5 my-3 mx-1.5" />
<p class="text-text-100 mt-3 -mb-1 text-[1.125rem] font-bold">Impact</p>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]">The campaign creative ran across national magazine placements and email marketing, reaching Art Carved&#8217;s broad customer base with imagery that was both brand-aligned and culturally forward.</p>
<hr class="border-border-200 border-t-0.5 my-3 mx-1.5" />

<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]">Print Art Direction · Email Design · Campaign Creative · Bridal &amp; Lifestyle Marketing</p>

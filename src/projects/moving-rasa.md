<h3 style="text-align: left;"><strong>Moving Rasa – Creative &amp; Digital Strategy</strong></h3>
<p style="text-align: left;"><strong>Client Overview:</strong><br />Moving Rasa is a movement-based healing organization focused on supporting marginalized communities—particularly Southeast Asian, Pacific Islander, Black, Indigenous, and differently abled individuals—through programs that center empowerment, wellness, and community care.</p>
<p style="text-align: left;"><strong>Scope of Work:</strong><br />As a creative consultant for Moving Rasa, I provided comprehensive support across digital strategy, content creation, and merchandise design:</p>
<ul style="text-align: left;">
<li>
<p><strong>Social Media Management:</strong><br />Researched culturally relevant topics and aligned messaging with community values. Designed and scheduled posts, edited video content, wrote engaging captions, and published across platforms to build visibility and audience engagement.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Digital Newsletter Design:</strong><br />Designed and managed bi-weekly email campaigns. This included layout design, content planning, and scheduling, ensuring each message reflected the organization&#8217;s tone, mission, and upcoming programming.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Merchandise Design &amp; Production:</strong><br />Led the concept-to-production process for branded merchandise. Conducted trend research, sourced materials, collaborated with vendors, and selected color palettes and garment styles that reflected Moving Rasa’s identity and community aesthetic.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p style="text-align: left;"><strong>Impact:</strong><br />This work supported Moving Rasa’s mission by amplifying their voice online, deepening community connection, and translating their values into tangible visual storytelling—both on screen and through wearable goods.&#8221;</p>

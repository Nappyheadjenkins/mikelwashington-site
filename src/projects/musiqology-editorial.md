<h1 class="text-text-100 mt-3 -mb-1 text-[1.375rem] font-bold">Musiqology — Editorial Design</h1>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]"><strong>Role:</strong> In-House Creative <strong>Scope:</strong> Website &amp; Editorial Design</p>
<hr class="border-border-200 border-t-0.5 my-3 mx-1.5" />
<h2 class="text-text-100 mt-3 -mb-1 text-[1.125rem] font-bold">Overview</h2>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]">Musiqology was a music and culture editorial platform built around the scholarship and artistry of Dr. Guthrie Ramsey and collaborating artists. As the in-house creative, I was responsible for designing the full digital editorial experience — from homepage to article layout.</p>
<hr class="border-border-200 border-t-0.5 my-3 mx-1.5" />
<h2 class="text-text-100 mt-3 -mb-1 text-[1.125rem] font-bold">The Work</h2>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]">I designed the end-to-end website experience for Musiqology, creating a platform that could present music journalism and cultural commentary with the visual sophistication the content deserved.</p>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]">The scope included homepage design, full site design, and article/content layout — each requiring its own creative thinking. The homepage had to draw readers in and signal the platform&#8217;s point of view immediately. Article layouts had to be clean and readable while still feeling distinctly Musiqology. Together, the site functioned as both a publication and a cultural statement.</p>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]">The editorial design system was built to be consistent enough to feel like a cohesive publication, and flexible enough to serve a wide range of content — essays, interviews, reviews, and artist features.</p>
<hr class="border-border-200 border-t-0.5 my-3 mx-1.5" />
<h2 class="text-text-100 mt-3 -mb-1 text-[1.125rem] font-bold">Legacy</h2>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]">The editorial framework developed for Musiqology laid the groundwork for the platform&#8217;s evolution into Musiqology Media, demonstrating the lasting impact of intentional design systems built for scale.</p>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]"><em>This project is part of a broader creative system developed for Musiqology — see also: Musiqology Brand Identity.</em></p>
<hr class="border-border-200 border-t-0.5 my-3 mx-1.5" />

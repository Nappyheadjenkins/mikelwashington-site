<h3>Musiqology — Brand Identity</h3>
<p><br />Role: In-House Creative Scope: Brand Identity Design</p>
<p><br /><strong>Overview</strong><br />Musiqology was an editorial platform celebrating music, culture, and scholarship — anchored by the work of Dr. Guthrie Ramsey and the artists in his orbit. Before the platform could publish a single word, it needed a visual identity that could carry the weight of that mission.</p>
<p><br /><strong>The Work</strong><br />I developed the brand identity system for Musiqology — logo, color palette, typography, and the overall look and feel — and extended that system into promotional graphics for Dr. Ramsey&#8217;s live shows and events.</p>
<p>That last part was critical. A brand that only lives on a website is incomplete — Musiqology needed to show up in the real world, on stage, and in the spaces where the music actually happened. I designed promotional materials that carried the brand&#8217;s visual language into event contexts, creating consistency across digital and physical touchpoints.</p>
<p>The design challenge throughout was finding the right balance: credible and culturally serious without being stiff, energetic without being shallow. The identity drew from the richness of Black music tradition while feeling contemporary and forward-looking.</p>
<p><br /><strong>Legacy</strong><br />Though the original platform is no longer active, the brand evolved into Musiqology Media — and the foundational identity work created during this period continues to inform the platform&#8217;s visual direction today.</p>
<p><br /><strong>Disciplines</strong><br />Brand Identity · Logo Design · Typography · Color Systems · Event Promotion · Print Design · Art Direction</p>

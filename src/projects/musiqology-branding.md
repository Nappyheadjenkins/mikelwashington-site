Musiqology was an editorial platform celebrating music, culture, and scholarship, anchored by the work of Dr. Guthrie Ramsey and the artists in his orbit. Before the platform could publish a single word, it needed a visual identity that could carry the weight of that mission.

I developed the brand identity system for Musiqology (logo, color palette, typography, and the overall look and feel) and extended that system into promotional graphics for Dr. Ramsey's live shows and events.

That last part was critical. A brand that only lives on a website is incomplete. Musiqology needed to show up in the real world, on stage, and in the spaces where the music actually happened. I designed promotional materials that carried the brand's visual language into event contexts, creating consistency across digital and physical touchpoints.

The design challenge throughout was finding the right balance: credible and culturally serious without being stiff, energetic without being shallow. The identity drew from the richness of Black music tradition while feeling contemporary and forward-looking.

Though the original platform is no longer active, the brand evolved into Musiqology Media, and the foundational identity work created during this period continues to inform the platform's visual direction today.

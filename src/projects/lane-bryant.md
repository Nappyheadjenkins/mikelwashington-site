<h1 class="text-text-100 mt-3 -mb-1 text-[1.375rem] font-bold">Lane Bryant</h1>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]"><strong>Role:</strong> In-House Art Director, Email Marketing <strong>Scope:</strong> Email Campaign Design &amp; Production</p>
<hr class="border-border-200 border-t-0.5 my-3 mx-1.5" />
<h2 class="text-text-100 mt-3 -mb-1 text-[1.125rem] font-bold">Overview</h2>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]">Lane Bryant is one of the most recognized names in plus-size fashion, serving millions of customers across the U.S. As an in-house Art Director, I led the design and production of email marketing campaigns that drove engagement and sales across the brand&#8217;s customer base.</p>
<hr class="border-border-200 border-t-0.5 my-3 mx-1.5" />
<h2 class="text-text-100 mt-3 -mb-1 text-[1.125rem] font-bold">The Work</h2>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]">I concepted, designed, and produced a high volume of promotional email campaigns — from seasonal pushes to time-sensitive flash sales. The work required balancing brand standards with the urgency of retail calendars, translating merchandising priorities into visual storytelling that moved customers to act.</p>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]">Every campaign had to feel fresh while staying unmistakably on-brand — bold, inclusive, and built for Lane Bryant&#8217;s loyal, fashion-forward audience.</p>
<hr class="border-border-200 border-t-0.5 my-3 mx-1.5" />
<h2 class="text-text-100 mt-3 -mb-1 text-[1.125rem] font-bold">Impact</h2>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]">One of my flash sale email designs proved so effective that it continued to be used by the team after my departure — a testament to the strength and versatility of the creative. Good design doesn&#8217;t clock out when you do.</p>
<hr class="border-border-200 border-t-0.5 my-3 mx-1.5" />

<h1 class="text-text-100 mt-3 -mb-1 text-[1.375rem] font-bold">Gloss.com</h1>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]"><strong>Role:</strong> Freelance Designer <strong>Scope:</strong> Email Marketing Design <strong>Client:</strong> Gloss.com (Estée Lauder)</p>
<hr class="border-border-200 border-t-0.5 my-3 mx-1.5" />
<h2 class="text-text-100 mt-3 -mb-1 text-[1.125rem] font-bold">Overview</h2>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]">Gloss.com was a dedicated e-commerce and editorial destination for Estée Lauder, one of the world&#8217;s most prestigious beauty conglomerates. The platform offered a curated digital shopping experience for prestige beauty — skincare, makeup, and fragrance across Estée Lauder&#8217;s portfolio of brands.</p>
<hr class="border-border-200 border-t-0.5 my-3 mx-1.5" />
<h2 class="text-text-100 mt-3 -mb-1 text-[1.125rem] font-bold">The Work</h2>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]">As a freelance designer, I was brought in to design marketing email campaigns for Gloss.com — translating the brand&#8217;s premium aesthetic into digital communications that drove engagement and purchase.</p>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]">Working within the visual standards of an Estée Lauder property meant delivering polished, high-end creative that met the expectations of a sophisticated beauty customer. Every email had to feel luxurious, on-brand, and shoppable.</p>
<hr class="border-border-200 border-t-0.5 my-3 mx-1.5" />
<h2 class="text-text-100 mt-3 -mb-1 text-[1.125rem] font-bold">Note</h2>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]">Gloss.com is no longer active, having since been folded back into Estée Lauder&#8217;s broader digital ecosystem — but the work represents experience designing for one of the most respected names in global beauty retail.</p>
<hr class="border-border-200 border-t-0.5 my-3 mx-1.5" />
<h2 class="text-text-100 mt-3 -mb-1 text-[1.125rem] font-bold">Disciplines</h2>
<p class="font-claude-response-body break-words whitespace-normal leading-[1.7]">Email Design · Freelance Creative · Beauty &amp; Retail Marketing · Digital Campaign Design</p>
